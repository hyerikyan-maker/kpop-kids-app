## 🚀 STEP 1: Go to Bing Image Creator

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox)
2. Go to: **bing.com/images/create**
3. You should see a page that says "Create with AI"
4. **NO SIGN-UP NEEDED!** Just start creating

---

## 📝 STEP 2: Create Main Zoey Image

### Copy this prompt exactly:

```
Create a cute, animated 7-year-old girl character named Zoey in K-pop style. She has long black hair with purple sparkly hair clips, big bright brown eyes, and a big genuine smile. She wears a purple crop top with silver sparkles, black shorts with silver trim, and white sneakers. She has purple and silver bracelets and a sparkly choker. Her skin is warm tan/brown. She's standing in a welcoming pose with her arms open, full of joy and energy. Animation style like Disney and Pixar, bright colors, professional illustration, made for kids content.
```

### How to use it:

1. Click in the text box (where it says "Join the creative revolution")
2. **Right-click** → **"Paste"** (or Ctrl+V / Cmd+V)
3. The prompt appears in the box
4. Click the **blue "Create" button**
5. Wait 1-2 minutes

---

## ⏳ STEP 3: Wait for Images

Bing will generate **4 different versions** of Zoey.

**This takes 1-2 minutes.**

- Image 1 (top left)

## 👀 STEP 4: Pick Your Favorite

Look at all 4 images. Which one looks most like Zoey?

**Good signs:**
- ✅ Big bright eyes
- ✅ Purple outfit visible
- ✅ Happy expression
- ✅ Kid-proportioned

If one looks GREAT, continue. If they all look "meh", no problem - regenerate!

## 💾 STEP 5: Download Your Image

**On the image you like:**

1. **Right-click** on the image
2. Click **"Save image as..."**
3. Choose where to save (Desktop is easiest)
4. **Change the filename to:** `zoey_main_standing.png`
5. Click **"Save"**

**Done!** ✅ You now have Zoey's main image!
