## ⏱️ 5-PHASE BREAKDOWN

---

## PHASE 1: PLANNING & PREP (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Gather Your Props
- [ ] Pick your outfit (K-pop style if possible, or colorful)
- [ ] Pick your location (bedroom, living room, outdoors)
- [ ] Clear any clutter from background
- [ ] Test lighting - do you look bright & clear?

### Step 2: Download Materials
- [ ] Download Zoey character image → Save to phone
- [ ] Download 30-second K-pop music → Save to phone
- [ ] Get the script from ZOEY_VOICE_SCRIPTS.md ready

### Step 3: Choreography Review
Read the dance steps:

**Dance: "Welcome Dance Challenge"**
1. **Step-Kick** (4 counts) - March in place 1-2-3, then kick!
2. **Arm Wave** (4 counts) - Wave right arm then left arm
3. **Spin** (4 counts) - Turn slowly around 360 degrees
4. **Jump** (4 counts) - Jump up and down celebrating

**Full combo:** Step-Kick → Arm Wave → Spin → Jump (do 2x)

### Step 4: Mental Practice
- Visualize yourself doing the dance
- Move through it slowly 1-2 times
- Don't worry about perfection!

---

## PHASE 2: FILMING (1-2 hours)

### Setup (15 minutes)

**Step 1: Lighting Check**
- Position yourself facing a window (if possible)
- OR place lamp to side (not directly in eyes)
- Film a 10-second test
- Check: Are you lit properly? Can people see your face?

**Step 2: Camera Position**
- Phone on tripod or propped up
- Frame: Head to toes visible
- Distance: 6-8 feet away
- Height: Phone at your chest level

**Step 3: Audio Check**
- Phone volume on
- No background TV/music
- Quiet room
- Film 10-second audio test

### Recording (45-60 minutes)

**You will film 3 sections separately:**

---

### SECTION A: Intro Shot (Record 3-5 times, pick best)

**Setup:**
- Stand facing camera
- Smile naturally
- Wave at camera

**What to do:**
- Look at camera lens (not at screen)
- Give natural wave
- Keep energy up
- Film for 5 seconds

**TAKES TO DO:**
1. First take (awkward, that's okay!)
2. Second take (more natural)
3. Third take (best one)

**SAVE:** Best take as `zoey_intro.mp4`

**Tips:**
- Don't overthink it
- Natural smile > fake smile
- Imagine friend on camera

---

### SECTION B: Dance Tutorial (Record 2-3 times slowly)

**Setup:**
- Stand in dance position
- Clear space around you

**What to do:**
- Do the dance SLOWLY (half speed)
- Exaggerate movements so clear
- Do: Step-Kick → Arm Wave → Spin → Jump
- Film entire sequence

**TAKES TO DO:**
1. First take (learn as you go)
2. Second take (more confident)

**SAVE:** Best take as `zoey_dance_slow.mp4`

**Tips:**
- Slow = better for learning
- Make each move BIG and clear
- Smile while dancing!
- Don't count out loud (add later)

---

### SECTION C: Full Energy Dance (Record 3-5 times at full speed)

**Setup:**
- Same as Section B

**What to do:**
- Do the FULL dance at normal speed
- Full energy and enthusiasm
- Celebrate at the end (jump, cheer, thumbs up!)

**TAKES TO DO:**
1. First take (just do it!)
2. Second take (better)
3. Third take (best one!)
4-5. More takes if you want

**SAVE:** Best take as `zoey_dance_full.mp4`

**Tips:**
- Have FUN with this
- Mess up = no big deal, just keep going
- Energy > Perfection
- Smile the whole time!

---

### SECTION D: Outro Shot (Record 2-3 times)

**Setup:**
- Face camera, happy expression
- Optional: hold thumbs up sign
- Optional: do peace sign

**What to do:**
- Smile at camera
- Give thumbs up or wave
- Film for 3-5 seconds

**SAVE:** Best take as `zoey_outro.mp4`

**Tips:**
- Make this warm & genuine
- Wink at camera (optional, cute!)
- Hold pose for 2 seconds

---

### Recording Tips for ALL Sections

**DO:**
✅ Film in natural lighting
✅ Look at camera lens (where videos are made!)
✅ Be energetic & happy
✅ Do multiple takes
✅ Keep each segment to 10-20 seconds

**DON'T:**
❌ Film in darkness
❌ Slouch or look bored
❌ Only do one take
❌ Stop mid-move (keep filming)
❌ Rush through it

## PHASE 3: VOICE RECORDING (20-30 minutes)

### Step 1: Prepare Script

You need Zoey to introduce the dance (this is her voice, not you!)

**Use this script from ZOEY_VOICE_SCRIPTS.md:**

> "Hi friends! I'm Zoey! Today we're learning an AMAZING dance! Are you ready to dance with me? Let's GO!"

### Step 2: Record Voice

**On iPhone:**
1. Open Voice Memos app
2. Click red record button
3. Speak in energetic, high voice (like 7-year-old girl)
4. Click stop when done
5. Share → Save to Files

**On Android:**
1. Open Google Recorder
2. Click red button
3. Speak clearly & energetically
4. Stop recording
5. Save file

### Step 3: Make It Sound Like Zoey

Read the script with:
- ✅ High, energetic voice
- ✅ Quick, enthusiastic pace
- ✅ Clear pronunciation
- ✅ Genuine excitement
- ✅ Smile while recording (can hear in voice!)

**DO NOT:**
- Deep voice
- Slow/bored tone
- Whispering
- Rush through

### Step 4: Do Multiple Takes

Record the intro line 3-5 times and pick the best one.

**SAVE:** Best take as `zoey_voice_intro.m4a`

## PHASE 4: EDITING IN CAPCUT (2-3 hours)

### Step 1: Download CapCut
- Go to capcut.com
- Download for your device
- Install & open

### Step 2: Create New Project
- Click "New Project"
- Choose: 1080 x 1920 (vertical - for YouTube Shorts!)
- Select your intro video (`zoey_intro.mp4`)

### Step 3: Build the Timeline

Your video will have these layers (from bottom to top):

```
LAYER 4: Text/Effects
LAYER 3: Zoey Images (side-by-side with your dance)
LAYER 2: Music (background)
LAYER 1: Your dance videos (main)
```

---

### BUILDING YOUR VIDEO STEP-BY-STEP

**Segment 1: Intro (0-3 seconds)**

1. Drag `zoey_intro.mp4` to timeline
2. Trim to 3 seconds
3. Add transition: "Pop" or "Whoosh"
4. Add text at bottom: "Welcome!"
5. Add music (starts here)

---

**Segment 2: Zoey Introduction (3-5 seconds)**

1. Add Zoey character image
2. Position on LEFT side of screen
3. Add sticker: "Play" button
4. Add voice recording of intro script
5. Add text: "Hi friends!"

---

**Segment 3: Dance Tutorial SLOW (5-15 seconds)**

1. Add your slow dance video to timeline
2. Add Zoey image on LEFT side
3. Add text: "Step-by-step!"
4. Add motion effects: "Zoom" to highlight moves
5. Keep music playing

---

**Segment 4: Full Speed Dance (15-25 seconds)**

1. Add your full-speed dance video
2. Add Zoey image on LEFT side (celebrating)
3. Add stickers: Stars, sparkles, hearts
4. Add text: "You got this!" (animated)
5. Music continues

---

**Segment 5: Celebration/Outro (25-30 seconds)**

1. Add your outro video
2. Add Zoey image (big smile)
3. Add confetti effect or celebration stickers
4. Add text: "You were AMAZING!"
5. Add music ending/fade out

---

### CapCut Basics Tutorial

**How to Add Video:**
1. Click "+" button
2. Select video from phone
3. Drag to timeline

**How to Add Music:**
1. Click "Music" tab
2. Search "K-pop upbeat"
3. Select royalty-free option
4. Drag to timeline

**How to Add Text:**
1. Click "Text" button
2. Type your text
3. Choose font (fun/colorful fonts!)
4. Position on screen
5. Adjust size

**How to Add Stickers:**
1. Click "Stickers"
2. Search "sparkles" "stars" "confetti"
3. Drag to video
4. Position & size

**How to Add Effects:**
1. Click "Effects"
2. Choose: "Pop", "Zoom", "Zoom Blur"
3. Add to video segment
4. Preview

**How to Preview:**
1. Click play button
2. Watch full video
3. Check timing matches music
4. Check text is readable
5. Check colors look good

### Common CapCut Issues & Fixes

**Problem: Audio out of sync**
- Fix: Trim beginning of music to match video start

**Problem: Video too slow/fast**
- Fix: Click video → Adjust speed slider

**Problem: Zoey image covers your face**
- Fix: Resize/move Zoey image to side

**Problem: Text too small**
- Fix: Select text → Drag corner to enlarge

**Problem: Video is sideways**
- Fix: Select video → Click rotate button

### Step 1: Export Video from CapCut

1. Click "Export"

3. Choose format: MP4
4. Click "Export"
5. Wait for rendering (2-5 minutes)
6. Save to phone when done

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Q: My video looks dark**
A: Retape in brighter location (window light)

**Q: Audio is too quiet**
A: Increase volume in CapCut before export

**Q: Video won't upload**
A: Check file size (under 500MB), try re-exporting

**Q: YouTube says "age-restricted"**
A: Re-upload and mark "Made for Kids" = YES

**Q: No views after 1 week**
A: Share the link everywhere + use hashtags + create more videos
