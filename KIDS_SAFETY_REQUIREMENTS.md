# 👶 Kids Safety & Legal Requirements

**IMPORTANT:** Before creating content for kids, you MUST follow these legal and safety guidelines.

---

## ⚠️ Legal Requirements (YouTube Kids Content)

### Mark Content as "Made for Kids"

When you upload each YouTube Short:
- [ ] Check the box: **"This video is made for kids"**
- [ ] YouTube will limit ads and tracking
- [ ] This is REQUIRED by US law (COPPA)

**Why it matters:** If you don't mark it correctly, you can get fined up to $43,000 per video.

### Age Restrictions

Your content is for:
- Children ages 4-9 (mostly)
- Requires parental permission for accounts under 13
- Mark all videos "Made for Kids"

---

## 🚫 Content You CANNOT Use

**Never include:**
- [ ] Violence or scary imagery
- [ ] Alcohol, drugs, or tobacco
- [ ] Inappropriate language
- [ ] Romantic/adult content
- [ ] Excessive advertising
- [ ] Personally identifiable info (names, addresses, phone numbers)
- [ ] Dangerous challenges
- [ ] Flashing lights (seizure risk)
- [ ] Copyrighted music without license

---

## ✅ Safety Checklist Before Publishing

### Video Content
- [ ] No fast flashing lights (can cause seizures)
- [ ] No scary faces or sounds
- [ ] No violence or fighting
- [ ] Dance moves are age-appropriate
- [ ] Messages are positive
- [ ] No product placement/advertising

### Audio
- [ ] Music is royalty-free (use Pixabay or YouTube Audio Library)
- [ ] Volume is moderate (not too loud)
- [ ] No curse words or inappropriate language
- [ ] Clear & easy to understand

### Text/Visuals
- [ ] Text is readable for kids (big font)
- [ ] Colors are not overwhelming
- [ ] Images are child-friendly
- [ ] No personal information visible

### Behavior Modeling
- [ ] Show positive behavior
- [ ] Model kindness & respect
- [ ] Demonstrate healthy habits
- [ ] Encourage creativity

---

## 🔒 Personal Privacy

### Don't Share:
- [ ] Your real name (use channel name instead)
- [ ] Your address or location
- [ ] Phone number or email
- [ ] School name or specific location
- [ ] Your child's personal details (if featuring kids)

### If Featuring Children:
- [ ] Get parent/guardian permission (in writing)
- [ ] Show only face and hands (not identifying details)
- [ ] Never show kids alone
- [ ] Have another adult present during filming
- [ ] Store videos securely

---

## 💬 Comment Moderation

### You MUST:
- [ ] Enable comment filtering
- [ ] Review comments before publishing
- [ ] Remove any inappropriate comments
- [ ] Never respond to predatory comments
- [ ] Report suspicious accounts

### Settings to Enable:
1. Go to YouTube Studio
2. Click "Settings"
3. Go to "Community"
4. Enable "Hold All Comments for Review"
5. Enable "Block Viewers"

### Block These Users:
- Anyone asking for personal info
- Inappropriate comments
- Spam
- Suspicious accounts
