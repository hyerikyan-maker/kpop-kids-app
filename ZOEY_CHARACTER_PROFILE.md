# 🌟 ZOEY - Character Profile

**Channel Host & Kids' Favorite Character**

---

## 👧 Who is Zoey?

Zoey is a 7-year-old K-pop loving character who hosts all the dances, challenges, and fun content on KpopWithElyse. She's enthusiastic, energetic, and makes every kid feel like they can be a K-pop star!

---

## 🎨 Visual Description

### Physical Appearance
- **Age:** 7-8 years old (character design)
- **Hair:** Long, silky black hair with cute bangs
- **Hair Accessories:** Purple sparkly hair clips or headband
- **Eyes:** Big, bright, expressive brown eyes
- **Skin Tone:** Warm tan/brown
- **Height:** Petite, kid-proportioned
- **Style:** K-pop inspired fashion

### Clothing (Main Outfit)
- **Top:** Purple or pink crop top with sparkles
- **Bottom:** Black or purple shorts with silver trim
- **Shoes:** White sneakers or purple dance shoes
- **Accessories:** 
  - Purple and silver bracelets
  - Purple sparkly choker
  - K-pop style badge/pin

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Purple (#8B4FB8)
- **Secondary:** Hot Pink (#FF1493)
- **Accent:** Silver/White (#F0F0F0)
- **Highlight:** Gold accents

---

## 😊 Personality Traits

### Energy Level
- **Always:** High energy, enthusiastic, happy
- **Never:** Tired, sad, or bored
- **Vibe:** Fun best friend who's always excited to dance

### Attitude
- ✅ Encouraging & supportive
- ✅ Positive & optimistic
- ✅ Fun & playful
- ✅ Inclusive (everyone can dance!)
- ✅ Patient & kind

### How She Speaks
- Friendly & casual
- Uses exclamation marks! A LOT!
- Says encouraging things
- Laughs genuinely
- Never talks down to kids
- Uses words kids understand

---

## 🎤 Voice & Tone

### Voice Type
- Bright, cheerful, high-pitched (like enthusiastic 7-year-old)
- Clear and easy to understand
- Natural and genuine (not overly cartoon-y)
- Energetic but not annoying

### Speaking Style
- Quick pace (kids follow better)
- Animated and expressive
- Lots of enthusiasm
- Genuine happiness in voice

### Example Lines
> "Hi friends! I'm Zoey! Today we're learning the COOLEST dance EVER!"

> "You're doing AMAZING! Keep going!"

> "This is SO FUN! Do you love it as much as I do?"

### Video Types & Roles

#### Dance Tutorial Videos
- **Role:** Main host & dancer
- **Position:** Center screen or side-by-side with host
- **Action:** Dances along, celebrates, encourages
- **Outfit:** Dance outfit (purple/pink theme)

#### Sing-Along Videos
- **Role:** Lead singer with host
- **Position:** Split screen or alternating
- **Action:** Sings words, points at audience, has fun
- **Outfit:** Singing outfit (sparkly, K-pop style)

#### Challenge/Game Videos
- **Role:** Game host & participant
- **Position:** Center to direct, then participates
- **Action:** Explains game, plays along, celebrates wins
- **Outfit:** Casual dance outfit

#### Story/Character Videos
- **Role:** Character telling story
- **Position:** Full screen or with background
- **Action:** Acts out story, shows emotions
- **Outfit:** Character-specific outfit
