# 💬 ZOEY VOICE SCRIPTS

All dialogue for Zoey across different video types. Read with high energy, genuine enthusiasm, and a bright voice like an excited 7-year-old!

---

## 📖 SCRIPT RECORDING TIPS

### How to Sound Like Zoey

**DO:**
- ✅ Use a higher pitch (don't strain, just naturally higher)
- ✅ Smile while recording (can hear happiness in voice!)
- ✅ Speak clearly and distinctly
- ✅ Use lots of expression
- ✅ Pause for emphasis
- ✅ Add genuine excitement
- ✅ Record multiple takes and pick the best

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Use a deep voice
- ❌ Sound bored or tired
- ❌ Mumble or speak unclearly
- ❌ Rush through lines
- ❌ Sound fake or over-animated
- ❌ Whisper or be too quiet

## 🎬 VIDEO TYPE 1: DANCE TUTORIAL

### Scene 1: Opening/Intro

**Duration:** 3-5 seconds  
**Tone:** Super excited, welcoming, warm

---

### INTRO - OPTION A (Recommended)

```

[Pause 1 second for effect]

ZOEY: "Today we're learning an AMAZING K-pop dance!"

[Build excitement]
```

## 🎙️ RECORDING INSTRUCTIONS

### Setup
1. Find quiet room
2. Plug in headphones (to hear yourself)
3. Open Voice Memos (iPhone) or Google Recorder (Android)
4. Position phone 6 inches from mouth

### Process
1. Read script once to warm up
2. Record Intro (try 3 times, pick best)
3. Record Middle section (try 2 times)
4. Record Outro (try 2-3 times)
5. Listen to each section
6. Re-record any section that sounds off

### Quality Checklist
- [ ] Voice is clear (not muffled)
- [ ] Energy is high (sounds genuine)
- [ ] No background noise (TV, music, traffic)
- [ ] Pacing is good (not rushed or too slow)
- [ ] Enthusiasm is real (not fake-sounding)

## 💡 PERFORMANCE TIPS

**For Maximum Zoey Energy:**

1. **Smile while recording** - Listeners can hear it!
2. **Stand up** - Gives more energy than sitting
3. **Move around** - Helps energy stay high
4. **Use hand gestures** - Even though not visible, affects voice
5. **Imagine kids listening** - Record like you're talking to 100 kids
6. **Take breaks** - Don't record all at once
7. **Listen to playback** - Hear how it sounds
8. **Re-record if needed** - Don't settle for "okay"
