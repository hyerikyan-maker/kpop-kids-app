## 🎤 TOOL 2: VOICE RECORDING

### iPhone Users: Voice Memos

1. Open "Voice Memos" app (built-in)
2. Tap red record button
3. Speak into phone
4. Tap stop
5. Tap "Done"
6. Select recording → tap "Edit"
7. Choose "Save" or "Delete"

**Export:**
1. Select recording
2. Tap share icon
3. Select "Files" or "Email"
4. Save to computer

### Android Users: Google Recorder

1. Install: Google Recorder (free app)
2. Open app
3. Tap blue record button
4. Speak
5. Tap stop

**Export:**
1. Open Google Recorder
2. Find recording
3. Tap share icon
4. Download to phone

## 📹 TOOL 4: CAPCUT VIDEO EDITOR

### Download CapCut (FREE)

1. Go to: capcut.com
2. Click "Download"
3. Choose your device (Windows/Mac/Mobile)
4. Install
5. Open
6. Click "New Project"

---

### CapCut Quick Tutorial

**Create New Video:**
1. Click "New Project"
2. Select video file (your filming)
3. Click "New video"

**Add Music:**
1. Click "Music" tab (bottom left)
2. Click "+" to add music
3. Select your MP3 file
4. Drag to timeline
5. Adjust volume

**Add Zoey Images:**
1. Click "Media" tab
2. Click "+" → "Images"
3. Select Zoey PNG files
4. Drag to timeline
5. Set duration (1-2 seconds each)

**Add Text:**
1. Click "Text" tab
2. Click "+"
3. Type your text
4. Position on video
5. Change font & color
6. Set duration

**Add Effects:**
1. Click "Effects" tab
2. Browse effects
3. Drag to clip you want
4. Adjust as needed

**Export Video:**
1. Click "Export"
2. Choose quality: "High" (720p)
3. Choose format: MP4
4. Choose resolution: 1080 x 1920 (vertical!)
5. Click "Export"
6. Wait for processing
7. Save to computer

**Full detailed guide:** See `FIRST_VIDEO_COMPLETE_GUIDE.md`

### CRITICAL: Mark as "Made for Kids"

1. Go to "More" → "Advanced settings"
2. Find: **"Is this video made for kids?"**
3. Select: **"YES"**
